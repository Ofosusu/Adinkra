# Adinkra Project Vision

## Mission

Help African Americans explore Ghanaian heritage, understand Ghana's
history and culture, and plan meaningful visits to the country.

## Intended Users

- African Americans researching family and cultural roots.
- Visitors interested in Ghanaian history and traditions.
- Travelers planning heritage and cultural trips.
- People interested in learning about Ghana before visiting.

## Product Principles

- Respect Ghanaian people, communities, histories, and traditions.
- Use reliable sources for historical and cultural information.
- Be transparent about tour information, prices, and availability.
- Design for mobile devices and accessibility.
- Protect visitor privacy and personal information.

## Initial Scope

Start with destination discovery, educational heritage content,
a tours overview, and a contact page.

Accounts, bookings, payments, and AI features are future possibilities,
not part of the current working website.
