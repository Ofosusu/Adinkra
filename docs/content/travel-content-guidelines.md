# Adinkra Travel Content Guidelines

## Destination records
Every destination should have:
- Name
- Region
- District or municipality
- Town or locality
- Category
- Description
- Cultural, historical or ecological significance
- Visitor activities
- GPS coordinates, when reliably sourced
- Current visitor access status
- Opening hours and fees, when confirmed
- Transport and accessibility information
- Facilities
- Official website or booking information
- Source URLs
- Date checked
- Verification status

## Verification
Use these labels:
- Verified
- Partially Verified
- Unverified — further research required

A place being historically documented does not mean it is open to visitors.

## Accommodation listings
Do not add a hotel or guesthouse until its identity, location, current operation,
photographs, contact details and booking link have been checked.

For each image, record its photographer, source and reuse permission.

## Weather
Monthly descriptions are general climate guidance, not a live forecast.
Weather varies between southern coastal Ghana, the forest belt and northern Ghana.

## Languages
Ghana has many languages. The current website list is a selection, not a
complete linguistic inventory. Check language names and classifications
against credible linguistic references before expanding it.
