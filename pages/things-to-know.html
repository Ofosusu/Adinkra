Things to Know Before Visiting Ghana

Practical information about getting around, weather, money and languages.

Getting Around Ghana

Ghana has several forms of transport. The best option depends on your destination, budget and comfort.

Ghana's Weather: January to December

Ghana is tropical, but weather differs between the coast, forest belt and northern savannah. These are general seasonal expectations, not a daily forecast.

Pack light clothing, comfortable walking shoes, sun protection and a light rain jacket. Check a local forecast before travelling.

Money and Currency in Ghana

The Ghanaian Cedi (GHS)

Ghana's official currency is the Ghanaian cedi, written as GHS and represented by GH₵. One cedi is divided into 100 pesewas. Prices in Ghana are normally quoted in cedis.

Banknotes and coins are used. Mobile money and bank cards are also used by many businesses, especially in cities. Cash is useful for markets, smaller shops, local transport and rural areas.

Some hotels, tour companies and businesses may quote prices in foreign currencies, but visitors should not assume that US dollars or other foreign currencies will be accepted by ordinary shops or transport operators. Exchange money through authorised providers.

Helpful Travel Reminders

Weather and clothing

Dress for heat and humidity, and carry rain protection during wetter months.

Local customs

Ask permission before photographing people, ceremonies, palaces or sacred places.

Planning journeys

Travel times can be longer than expected. Allow extra time for traffic and road conditions.

Payments

Carry some cedis in smaller denominations, even if you also use cards or mobile money.
