Festivals of Ghana

Festivals are a living part of Ghanaian life. They mark harvests, history, thanksgiving and community. Many follow traditional calendars, so dates change every year.

Dates here are general. Confirm each year's date and visitor etiquette before you plan a trip. Ask before taking photographs at ceremonies.
