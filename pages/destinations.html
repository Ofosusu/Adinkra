Destinations in Ghana

Ghana has 16 regions. Each one has its own history, landscapes and traditions. Choose a region to see its places.

Before you go: Opening hours, fees and access change. Some historic forts are temporarily closed for conservation. Always confirm current details with the site or the Ghana Museums and Monuments Board before you travel. Places marked "Unverified" are leads that still need research.
